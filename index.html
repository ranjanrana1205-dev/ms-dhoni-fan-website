01 / FOR INDIA

He gave a nation belief.

As India's captain, Mahi turned pressure into possibility and led a golden generation with an unshakable calm.

  • 2007 World T20 champions
  • 2010, 2016 Asia Cup wins
  • 2011 Cricket World Cup champions
  • 2013 Champions Trophy winners
02 / THE CAREER

A complete cricketer.

India's fearless wicketkeeper-batter became one of cricket's greatest finishers, captains, and decision-makers.

  • 350 ODI appearances for India
  • 17,266 international runs
  • 634 international dismissals
  • 332 matches as India's captain
